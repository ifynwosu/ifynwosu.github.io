Core Capabilities & Research Focus

Translating high-throughput sequencing data into actionable biological insights.

🧬 High-Throughput RNA-Seq & Variant Discovery

End-to-end alignment (BWA, Bowtie2, STAR, Minimap2), variant calling (GATK best-practices, SAMtools, BCFTools), and expression quantification across diverse biological genotypes.

📊 Transcriptomics & Co-Expression Networks

Cross-study meta-analyses, differential expression (DESeq2, edgeR), and Weighted Gene Co-expression Network Analysis (WGCNA) for biomarker and trait identification.

🤖 Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling

Supervised ML classification architectures (Scikit-Learn, PyTorch) for predicting biological subtypes, complex traits, and drug-induced responses.

🧪 Wet-Lab Molecular & HMW Extractions

High Molecular Weight (HMW) DNA extraction, nucleic acid isolation (plant & tissue), PCR, gel electrophoresis, and sequencing library preparation.

🖥️ Linux HPC & Server Infrastructure

High-Performance Computing cluster management (SLURM), parallelized bash automation, Docker containerization, and reproducibility.

🌱 Plant Genomics & Cassava Improvement

Genome-wide coding region analysis, cassava mosaic virus resistance gene discovery, and pangenome graph concepts in crop science.
