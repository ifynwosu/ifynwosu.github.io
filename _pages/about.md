I am a **Computational Biologist and Bioinformatician** with a Ph.D. in Biology (Bioinformatics specialization) from Brigham Young University. My research focuses on transcriptomic meta-analyses, machine learning architectures for high-throughput gene-expression data, plant genomics, and RNA-Seq variant discovery pipelines.

### Research Interests
- **Genomics & Transcriptomics:** Reference & *de-novo* genome assembly, RNA-Seq SNP discovery workflows (GATK best-practices), differential expression, gene co-expression networks (WGCNA).
- **Machine Learning & Meta-Analysis:** Supervised ML architectures for complex genomic trait prediction and cross-study transcriptomic integration.
- **Molecular Biology & Wet-Lab:** High Molecular Weight (HMW) DNA extraction, nucleic acid isolation, and sequencing library preparation.
